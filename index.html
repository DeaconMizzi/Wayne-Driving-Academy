About Us

Built on Experience, Focused on Safer Roads

We are a recently established driving academy with fifteen years of experience. By ensuring that our students receive the best instruction, practice, and experience, we aim to make roads safer for everyone.

Wayne has always had a passion for cars, which inspired him to become a driving instructor. Over time, he saw road risks increasing and committed himself to raising teaching standards for every student.

As owner of Wayne Driving Academy, he continues to set high expectations so every student develops strong driving knowledge, practical ability, and the confidence needed for everyday road situations.
